Feedstock-Related Anomalies

    Seasonal variation in manure composition → changes in TS/VS or C:N ratio.
    High nitrogen content → ammonia inhibition, especially with poultry manure.
Environmental Anomalies

Temperature shocks:
    Cold waves (sudden drop in winter nights).
    Heat waves (digesters overheating above 45–50 °C).

Seasonal humidity affecting microbial activity.

Process Anomalies
    Gas leakage from balloon/storage system.
