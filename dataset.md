Feedstock Parameters:
    Poultry manure (20% of mix)
    Seed material: 70% cow manure + 10% anaerobic sludge
    Water added in 1:1 ratio (to achieve slurry)
    Total solids (TS) of poultry manure: ~85% (slurry 12%)
    Volatile solids (VS) content: ~88%
    C:N ratio: ~32 (mixture ~30)
    Chemical oxygen demand (COD): ~70,000 mg/L (poultry manure)
    Total Kjeldahl Nitrogen (TKN): ~503 mg/L

Operational Parameters
    Digester volume: 0.5 m³ (working volume ~0.3 m³)
    Organic loading rate (OLR): 2.5 → 6 kg TVS/m³·d
    Feeding frequency: once per day (after startup)
    Retention: daily loading of ~18 kg manure
    Mixing: periodic manual mixing with iron agitators
    Heating: solar hot water system (mesophilic 30–45 °C)
    Process Monitoring Parameters

Daily:
    pH (start ~7.2–7.4, later ~7.9)
    Temperature (°C)
    Biogas volume (L/day)

Weekly:
    Methane concentration (% CH₄)
    Volatile fatty acids (VFA, mg/L HAc)
    Alkalinity (mg/L CaCO₃ equivalent)

Occasional:
    Gas composition (CH₄, CO₂, O₂)
    Digestate nutrient content (N, P, K)
    Pathogen counts (E. coli, Streptococcus faecalis, coliforms)

Output Parameters
    Total biogas production: 39.95 m³/year

Inputs (features): TS, VS, C:N ratio, OLR, temp, pH, VFA, alkalinity, retention time, feed composition, solar heating.

Outputs (targets): Biogas yield (m³/day)
